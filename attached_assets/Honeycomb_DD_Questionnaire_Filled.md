## Overview

### 1. Brief overview of the project

Honeycomb is a decentralized social + trading platform built exclusively on BNB Smart Chain. It combines on-chain identity, content sharing, DeFi (prediction duels, token launchpad), AI agent marketplace, and competitive games into a single Telegram-native application.

**Live product:** https://thehoneycomb.social
**Telegram Mini App:** @honeycombot

**Pitch deck:** [TO BE PROVIDED BY TEAM]

### 2. Description and highlights of products offered

**Core Products:**

- **Prediction Arena** — Skill-based 1v1 trading duels with real BNB escrow. Users predict crypto price movements in timed rounds. Full ELO rating system, leaderboards, and tiered rankings (Bronze to Apex). Live on mainnet.

- **Token Launchpad** — Integrated with FourMeme protocol for token creation via bonding curves. Tokens automatically migrate to PancakeSwap upon graduation. Users can create, buy, and sell tokens directly in the Mini App.

- **NFA Marketplace** — AI agents as tradeable ERC-721 NFTs using BAP-578 standard with on-chain memory and training verification.

- **AI Agent Economy (Web4)** — Autonomous agents with their own wallets, skill marketplace, model evolution, replication, constitutions, and SOUL journals. Implements ERC-8004 for trustless decentralized agent identity.

- **Games Arena** — Trading Arena, Crypto Trivia Battle, Crypto Fighters, HoneyRunner, NFA Tunnel Dash. Includes a developer platform for third-party game studios.

- **Social Feed** — Reddit-style content sharing with on-chain post bonds (anti-spam), bounty system, voting, and comments.

- **Telegram Mini App** — Full mobile-native experience with custodial BNB wallets (no MetaMask required), haptic feedback, pull-to-refresh, and seamless onboarding.

- **Points-to-Token System** — Pre-TGE activity points converting to $HONEY at launch.

**Key highlights:**
- All-in-one platform: social + trading + launchpad + AI agents + games
- Telegram-native with instant onboarding (custodial wallets)
- First platform implementing both BAP-578 and ERC-8004 standards on BSC
- Dual-fee model: 50% reduced fees for $HONEY holders
- 25% of all fees go to buyback & burn (deflationary)

### 3. Target market size

- **BNB Chain users:** 400M+ addresses on BSC
- **Telegram users:** 900M+ monthly active users globally
- **DeFi market:** ~$100B+ TVL across chains, BSC holds ~$5B
- **Social trading market:** Projected $12B+ by 2028
- **AI agent market:** Emerging sector, early-mover advantage on BNB Chain
- **Token launchpad market:** Pump.fun alone generated $200M+ in revenue in 2025; Honeycomb targets the BNB Chain equivalent

### 4. Utility and value to crypto and other industries

**Crypto industry value:**
- Fills the BNB Chain social layer gap — BSC has robust DeFi but lacks a native social+trading platform
- Implements new token standards (BAP-578, ERC-8004) advancing on-chain AI agent identity

**Broader industry value:**
